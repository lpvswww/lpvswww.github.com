About Me

I am a recommendation algorithm engineer working at Tencent since 2017, focusing on Recommendation Algorithms and Reinforcement Learning. Our team has lots of practical experience in RS (Recommendation System) and has proposed many innovative ideas, achieving significant benefits. Some of our solutions have been made public and have been implemented in multiple large-scale RSs. The thoughts in our public papers have also inspired related works in the industry.

We have a profound understanding of RSs and we are very interested in innovative ideas that have practical value, rather than ideas that seem complex and grandiose, but have no practical value. Because we believe that only ideas which have value in practice are good ideas. We welcome communication and discussion of ideas via email (liupengvswww@gmail.com or liupengvswww@163.com) (Here is a link to my Google Scholar.)
